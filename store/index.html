CHS Cru Store sales dashboard

Knowing when to order, before we run out.

High School Ministry sells a handful of products through the Cru Store: THE FOUR bands, Soularium playing cards, the Reach Your School Playbook and a few books and guides. Every month the store sends a report. For a long time that report was the only thing I had, and reading it was not the same as understanding it.

Before

I never had a real grasp on my numbers. I was reactive instead of thinking ahead, so I did not know what was going on or what our prices really were.

We ran out of product in the store more than once. Getting it back on the shelf took weeks, sometimes months. We lost the sales, we looked inconsistent, and it hurt our relationship with the people buying from us.

Now

It updates itself from each month's report, so I stay in the know without setting time aside for it. I do not have a lot of time to give this.

It tells me when it is time to order, and we stop getting behind.

The one that changed how I work. The reorder table is the whole reason this exists. Before it, I found out we were out of something when somebody told me. Now I can see it coming three months out, which is about how long it takes to get product back on the shelf.

How it stays current

1

The report arrives

The Cru Store emails a consignment report each month with what sold.

2

It gets filed and read

The numbers go into the dashboard's own data, alongside four years of history.

3

The maths redoes itself

Sales pace, months of stock left and net per item all recalculate.

4

I look at one table

If nothing is near its reorder point, I close it. That is most months.
