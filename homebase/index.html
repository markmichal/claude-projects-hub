Home Base

Thinking it through in the car.

A small app on my phone's home screen, holding the little tools I build with Claude that are not worth a whole app of their own. The star of it is the Parking Lot: where an idea goes to get thought through, usually while I am driving.

44 seconds, no talking, built in the Blackout theme. The written version is below.

The problem

Ideas turn up while I am driving, halfway through a meeting, or almost asleep. Never at a desk.

And the ones I did write down sat there as a title and nothing else. I would open one at my desk and have to start thinking about it from scratch, which is exactly when I had the least time to.

How I work now

An idea goes in the Carport the second it shows up. Then, driving, I open one and talk it through with Claude out loud. Claude asks questions, I make the calls, and every word gets written down.

By the time I park, the idea is decided, so it moves to the Parking Lot. At my desk I open it and we build. The thinking already happened, in the one part of my day with nothing else pulling at me.

Try the screens

The real app, running on made-up ideas. Tap the Parking Lot card, switch to the Carport, open an idea. Nothing is saved and no real list is touched.

The whole point

Driving turned into thinking time

I cannot type in the car, but I can talk, and I have nobody interrupting me. Talking an idea through with Claude for twenty minutes gets further than an hour at my desk between emails.

Why two lists

Carport, then Parking Lot

The Carport holds a thought I have not worked out yet. The Parking Lot holds one I have, decisions and all. Moving a card from one to the other is how I know it is ready.

Why Notion

One list, not a second one

The app does not store anything of its own. It reads and writes the Idea Parking Lot I already had, so Claude in the car and Claude at my desk are looking at the same thing.

The other tools

A home for small things

A camera for designs I like, and a story library. Little tools I wanted on my phone without building a whole separate app for each one. Home Base is where they live.

Not in the demo: swiping a row, the quick add box, the camera, and the story library. Those need the real app.

How it is put together

1

A web page, saved to the home screen

No app store. It is a website that behaves like an app once you add it to your phone.

2

A relay in the middle

A small server holds the Notion key, so the key never sits on the phone where anyone could read it.

3

Locked to one person

Sign in happens before anything loads, and the relay checks the email again on its own side.

4

Notion stays the truth

Nothing is stored twice. Change an idea anywhere and every view agrees.

Why this one matters to me. It is not really about catching ideas. It is that my commute stopped being dead time. I get more done now, and the thinking is better, because it happens somewhere quiet.
