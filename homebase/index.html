Home Base

Catching an idea before it goes.

A small app that lives on my phone's home screen. One tool so far: a clean front end for the list of ideas I already keep in Notion.

32 seconds, no talking. The written version is below.

The problem

Ideas turn up while I am driving, halfway through a meeting, or almost asleep. Never at a desk.

By the time I could write one down properly, it was gone. Not forgotten exactly, just never written anywhere I would look again.

How I work now

Two taps from the home screen and it is down, in the same list Claude reads. An idea caught in the car can be a thing I am building that week.

Try the screens

The real app, running on made-up ideas. Tap the Parking Lot card, switch to the Carport, open an idea. Nothing is saved and no real list is touched.

Why two lists

Carport, then Parking Lot

A half formed thought and a ready one need different homes. Carport is for the fuzzy ones I still need to talk through. The Parking Lot is for the ones ready to build.

Why Notion

One list, not a second one

The app does not store anything of its own. It reads and writes the Idea Parking Lot I already had, so Claude in a chat and Claude in my editor are looking at the same thing.

Why a next step

Every idea carries its next move

"Sketch the two screens" is more use than a title on its own. When I have twenty minutes, I can see which one I could actually start.

Added later

A camera for designs I like

A yellow camera button. Snap a design, say what you like about it out loud, and it saves with the photo. Claude reads those before it makes anything visual for me.

Not in the demo: swiping a row, the quick add box, the camera, and the story library. Those need the real app.

How it is put together

1

A web page, saved to the home screen

No app store. It is a website that behaves like an app once you add it to your phone.

2

A relay in the middle

A small server holds the Notion key, so the key never sits on the phone where anyone could read it.

3

Locked to one person

Sign in happens before anything loads, and the relay checks the email again on its own side.

4

Notion stays the truth

Nothing is stored twice. Change an idea anywhere and every view agrees.

Why this one matters to me. I build things to take friction out of my week, and losing ideas was the friction I kept hitting. This is the one I open most.
