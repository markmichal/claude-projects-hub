Cru Theme Lab

Thirteen slide themes, ranked.

Every talk, training and student night needs slides. Mine were scattered across old decks and whatever I threw together that week. So I had Claude build thirteen full Cru branded themes, and then I put them in order from favourite to least. The order is the clever part: Claude reads it and the next batch comes back closer to what I actually like.

Before

Slide themes scattered, hit or miss. Every new deck started from a blank page or from whatever old file I could find, and nothing looked like it belonged to the same ministry.

Now

Thirteen finished themes in one place, in my order. I pick one, grab its sample, and Claude builds the deck in that look. Ranking them teaches Claude my taste, so each new round lands closer.

How it works

1

Claude builds a batch

Each theme is a real seven slide deck: cover, section break, statement, photo, three points, big number, close.

2

I put them in order

Arrows move a theme up or down. Ones I do not like get archived with a reason, like "too corporate" or "hard to read".

3

Claude learns the taste

The next batch leans toward what my top three have in common and steers away from the archive reasons.

4

A favourite becomes a skill

When one is exactly right, it gets saved as a skill. Then "make it in Classroom" works in any chat, for any format.

Why the ranking matters more than the themes. Any of these could have been a one off. Putting them in an order turns a pile of designs into something Claude can learn from, and that is the part I would build again.
